# Privacy Policy — PDF PLUS (PDF Maker Plus)

**Package Name:** `com.pdfplus.maker`
**Effective Date:** November 2026

## 1. Local-Only Document Processing
PDF PLUS processes all PDF documents, photos, scanned pages, signatures, watermarks, and `.docx`/text files **100% locally on your device**. Your files are never uploaded to external servers, analyzed in the cloud, or shared with third parties.

## 2. Permissions & Storage Access
- **Android Photo Picker & Storage Access Framework (SAF):** PDF PLUS accesses only the specific photos, documents, or save destinations you explicitly select through system pickers. We do not request broad storage access permissions.
- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Requested strictly when you use the live PDF Scanner feature, and only after your explicit runtime consent. You can also scan/import images via the zero-permission Photo Picker.

## 3. Data Storage
Documents you create or import are stored inside your app's private internal storage (`filesDir/pdf_vault/`) and indexed locally in an on-device Room database. Uninstalling the app removes private vault files unless you have exported them to your chosen folder via Download.

## 4. Contact
For support or privacy inquiries, please contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing for **PDF PLUS**.
